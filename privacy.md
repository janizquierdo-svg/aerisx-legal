# Aeris Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 30 September 2026_

Aeris is a camera and photo-editing app for iPhone. This policy explains what the app does with your information. The short version: **Aeris has no accounts, no servers, no analytics and no ads. Your photos, videos and settings stay on your iPhone.**

## What Aeris collects

Nothing is collected by us. Aeris does not send your photos, videos, location, or any other personal data to the developers or to any third party. The app contains no third-party analytics, advertising or tracking code, and it does not track you across other apps or websites.

## Permissions the app asks for, and why

- **Camera and microphone** — to take photos and record video with sound. Everything is processed on your iPhone.
- **Photos library** — to save your photos and videos, show them in the app's gallery, and save your edits back to them. When a photo is stored in iCloud Photos, Apple's Photos framework may download the original from your own iCloud account so it can be displayed or edited. That exchange is between your iPhone and Apple.
- **Location (optional)** — when you allow it and Settings → Shooting → Location is on, Aeris adds the place a photo or video was taken to that item in your Photos library, the way the built-in Camera app does. Location is only used while the app is open, and it is never sent anywhere. When you share a photo from inside Aeris, the location is not written into the shared file. You can turn this off in the app's Settings or in iOS Settings at any time.

## What stays on your device

To let you re-edit a shot later, Aeris keeps an unedited copy of photos taken with a look, plus the settings of your edits, in the app's private storage on your iPhone. This copy is excluded from iCloud backups, is never uploaded, and is deleted when you delete the app. Your photos and videos in the Photos library are not affected by deleting the app.

Features such as scene suggestions, subject detection and Portrait effects use Apple's on-device frameworks. The images they analyse never leave your iPhone.

## Crash reports and beta feedback

If you have turned on **Share with App Developers** in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Analytics & Improvements, Apple may share anonymous crash and performance reports with us. If you test a beta through TestFlight, Apple shares the crash reports, screenshots and comments you choose to send. We use these only to fix problems in Aeris. Apple's handling of this data is covered by Apple's privacy policy.

## Contacting support

If you email us, we receive your email address and whatever you write. We use it only to reply to you, and we do not share it.

## Children

Aeris is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from anyone.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.
